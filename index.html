Methodology & boundaries

01 / DATA

One declared dataset

Quoted closes support market monitoring. Separate, consistently defined total-return indices support strategy returns. Observation dates, availability times and source identities travel with the data.

02 / MODEL

Explicit research assumptions

The sector model combines macro sensitivities with six-month momentum. Its fixed coefficients are not company fundamentals, a calibrated probability or a demonstrated investment edge.

03 / EXECUTION

Timing and costs matter

Monthly decisions precede execution. The engine checks warm-up and coverage, charges signed turnover and financing, and reports actual evaluation dates. Missing required inputs block a simulation.

Reading a run: parameters, dataset identity, model version, dates and assumptions are saved together in each result. Undefined metrics are shown as unavailable with their reason. Drawdown reflects the engine's observation frequency and does not establish intraday margin survival.

Ranked three assigns the largest short weight to the worst-ranked sector. Strategy comparisons use the same dataset and requested assumptions.
